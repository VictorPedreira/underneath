A mature operation with a large, established website, but with visibility problems that limited new contacts from organic search.

momentvmopvs.com

The starting point

The site already existed, with extensive content and pages published long ago — but most of the traffic still depended on referrals and social media. The 4-layer diagnosis revealed gaps in findability and relevance: parts of the site weren't being indexed correctly, and important pages didn't clearly answer the searches that already existed in the industry.

What was fixed

  • Crawling and indexing fixes on the pages with the most search potential
  • Architecture and URLs reorganized around the real intent of the people searching
  • Technical performance and structured data adjustments
  • Content rewritten to directly answer the target audience's questions

Approach

Since the site was already live and partly indexed, the fixes were made on top of the existing foundation — no rebuild from scratch. The focus was identifying exactly which of the four layers (findability, relevance, trust or action) was holding back each page, and fixing them in the right order, following the UNDERNEATH method.
