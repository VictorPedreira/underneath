New site Structure from the start

Zavati Estamparia

A young print shop with no previous website, building its online presence from scratch — with the chance to apply the right structure before any visibility problem appeared.

zavatiestamparia.com

The starting point

No previous site, no indexing history and no technical debt to fix. The challenge here wasn't diagnosing a problem but preventing one: applying the 4 layers — findability, relevance, trust and action — from the first published page, focused on attracting corporate and school uniforms, the business's priority segment.

What we applied

  • Page architecture designed from the start for the segment's search intent (corporate and school uniforms)
  • Correct indexing from launch, with no orphan or duplicate pages
  • Structured data and trust signals implemented from launch
  • Content written to answer the audience's real questions, not to fill up the site

Approach

Since nothing had been published before, the right structure could be applied completely and in the right order, following the UNDERNEATH method — with no need to undo earlier technical decisions.
