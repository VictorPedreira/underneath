When a business isn't found, the problem is always in one of these 4 layers

Findability, relevance, trust and action. The UNDERNEATH diagnosis examines each one on your current site before any fix.

Findability

Is the site discovered, crawled and indexed by Google? This is the basics — without it, none of the other layers matter. It's as if the page didn't exist.

Relevance

Does the page answer exactly what people are searching for? Keywords scattered across the site aren't enough — the structure and content must match the real intent of the person searching.

Trust

Are there enough signals of authority and legitimacy? This includes structured data, social proof and the technical signals search engines and AIs use to decide whether to show your business.

Action

Do visitors understand what to do once they find your site? Findability without conversion is wasted traffic — the page needs to guide the next step clearly.

How the diagnosis works

Before any fix, we map the 4 layers on your site as it stands today — no guesswork. What this check usually covers:

  • Crawling, indexing and URL architecture
  • Match between pages and real search intent
  • Structured data, performance and authority signals
  • Navigation clarity and calls to action

The result shows exactly which layer holds the problem — before any sales conversation.
