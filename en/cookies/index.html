In short

  • The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising pixels or other tracking tools.
  • It only uses 2 temporary entries in the browser tab’s memory, deleted right after use, to control animations.
  • That is why there is no consent banner: there is nothing optional to accept or reject.
  • If this changes, nothing optional will run before you choose.

1. What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser to recognise it later. Similar technologies, such as local storage, session storage and pixels, can have comparable effects. Calling an external service, such as the form delivery service, does not necessarily mean a cookie is set.

2. What we found on the site

On 29 October 2026, we tested 10 pages in Portuguese and English in the Chrome browser, checking cookies, local storage, session storage and every request the pages made. The result: no cookies and no local storage items. These are the technologies in use:

How technical access data is handled is explained in our Privacy Policy. If our hosting or site protection provider starts setting security cookies, they will be added to this table.

3. Why there is no cookie banner

Consent banners exist to ask for your permission before optional technologies, such as audience analytics or advertising, are switched on. Since the site uses none of them, there is no choice to make, and a banner would only get in the way. The resources listed above are needed for the site to work and are disclosed here openly.

4. If this changes

If we decide to use an optional tool in the future, before switching it on we will:

  • update this policy with the name, provider, purpose, duration and legal basis of each technology;
  • show a notice with Accept, Reject and Customise options, equally prominent and with nothing pre-selected;
  • keep the tool blocked until you choose, without stopping you from reading the site if you reject it;
  • add a permanent cookie settings link to the footer so you can change or withdraw your choice at any time.

5. Controlling this in your browser

You can block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings. The 2 session storage entries delete themselves right after use and disappear anyway when you close the tab. Blocking these resources does not stop you from reading the content; it only turns off the animation transitions.

6. Links to other sites

When you follow links to WhatsApp, Instagram, X or client websites, you leave our site, and those services apply their own cookie and privacy policies.
