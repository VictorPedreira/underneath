Zero-click: when Google answers and nobody clicks

More and more searches end on Google's own results page: the answer appears ready-made and the user doesn't need to click anything. That doesn't mean being found has lost its value — it means the goal has changed.

UNDERNEATHOctober 25, 20263 min read

What a zero-click search is

It's a search that gets resolved without the user visiting any site. AI-generated answers, featured snippets, business panels, forecasts, conversions and maps deliver the information right on the results page. For simple questions, the ready-made answer is enough — and the click never happens.

For website owners, the effect shows up in the numbers: impressions continue, sometimes even grow, but clicks don't keep up.

If nobody clicks, what is still worth it?

The competition becomes about being the business remembered in the answer. When your brand name appears cited, repeated and associated with the topic, users keep that reference — and come back to it when they're ready to hire, often searching for the business by name.

This is what has been called synthetic authority: the set of technical and content signals that make AI systems associate your brand with a topic and mention it in generated answers.

Information gain: what Google now rewards

With the internet flooded with mass-generated text, content that repeats what already exists has lost value. The concept of information gain describes the opposite: content that adds something readers wouldn't find on other pages about the same topic. Google's guidelines on helpful content point in the same direction.

  • Proprietary data: numbers from your operation, client results, your own research.
  • The practitioner's perspective: what works and what doesn't in the day-to-day of your service.
  • Real cases: before and after, processes, decisions and lessons learned.
  • Signed opinion: a clear position, with an identified author.
In practice

In Google Search Console, compare the impressions and clicks of your pages. Pages with many impressions and few clicks show where the answer is already being given in search — and where it's worth reinforcing your brand presence and exclusive content.

How to adapt

  • Track searches for your business name: they measure the recall that zero-click visibility generates.
  • Answer questions directly and completely, with your brand name in context.
  • Publish data and experiences only your business has.
  • Use structured data so generated answers understand who the source is.
  • Send the visitors who do click to a page with a clear next step — people who arrive after the ready-made answer are already more decided.
In the 4 UNDERNEATH layers

Zero-click search weighs most on the relevance and trust layers: answering better than other sources and having the authority to be cited. But without findability the brand doesn't even enter the race, and without action the click that does happen is lost.
